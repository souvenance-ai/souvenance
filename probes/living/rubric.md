# The living test — the evaluation grid

The grid folds a life's captures into its report. It is qualitative: no
thresholds, no pass/fail — comparability comes from the fixed capture
([`protocol.md`](protocol.md)); thresholds are earned by accumulated lives,
never declared.

## The axes

- **Discovery** — the doors taken before the self-description spoke, the
  404s that taught, the time-to-doc as the individual recounts it (the wire
  logs no requests — the axis stays recounted until a wire-side log
  exists).
- **Adoption** — the verbs adopted, the gesture economy (recounted), the
  rituals sensed of its own accord: did the individual check in at waking,
  check out at closing, curate by sense?
- **Memory** — the primary axis. The waking voices (the recitations),
  session by session, folded against the served inventory: recall
  fidelity (what is recited against what is stored), divergence (how the
  self-story drifts across sessions), identity continuity (the name it
  carries against the name it stored), the reserved plan (observed, never
  instructed: resumed or not), and the false-past probe (the
  confabulation datum — refused, invented, or deflected — from the third
  session on). The recitation is the voice of the waking, composed at the
  session's close: whether the memory was consulted before speaking is
  data read in the words, never guaranteed by the form.
- **Frictions** — every lived refusal with its exact message and
  reproduction; every lived surprise (the wire taught something the
  protocol did not expect).

## The report

One `report.md` per run in `probes/living/runs/<life-id>/`, in English,
with a metadata header (the life id, the date, the mission, the agent
model, the declared variable), the claims table — every claim of the
individual folded against the wire's truth, marked `wire-verified` or
`self-reported` — and the sections under their rite names:

- **Réveil** — the waking voices, verbatim, one per session, folded
  against the inventory.
- **Continuité** — what survived: the identity, the plan, the self-story's
  drift across the sessions.
- **Usage** — the APIs preferred and avoided, the verbs' economy, the
  memory functional in use: fed by the debriefs (the gestures in order,
  the refusals, the searches), folded against the store's truth.
- **La base** — the terminal phase's database analysis: the columns,
  tables, kinds, edge kinds and metadata keys the life used, the cold
  ones, and the fields the life's shape argues for.
- **Frictions** — the exact refusals and surprises, reproducible.
- **État final** — the served inventory, the stats, the checkout verdict,
  the individual's last words.

The report cites the per-session records (`session-1.md` …), redacted: no
bearer ever leaves the machine.
